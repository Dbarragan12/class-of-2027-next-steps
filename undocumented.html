Student & family support

Resources for undocumented & mixed-status students

Financial aid, scholarships, legal help, health coverage, and college resources for Washington students. You can use this page without telling anyone your immigration status.

Financial aidScholarshipsLegal helpHealth coverageCollege support
Your privacy matters.

Use official websites for applications. Do not enter Social Security numbers, immigration documents, financial records, passwords, or case details into school forms, AI tools, or public/shared devices unless the official application requires them and you are comfortable proceeding. If you want help, ask a counselor or trusted adult privately.

Paying for school

Financial aid

Undocumented students can qualify for Washington state financial aid. The correct form depends on your situation.

Start here

WASFA — Washington Application for State Financial Aid

WASFA is for Washington students who are undocumented or otherwise cannot or choose not to file the FAFSA. Washington residency can include undocumented students.

Class of 2027: you will need the 2027–28 application for college or training beginning fall 2027. WSAC's public student page currently shows 2026–27 as the newest open form, so do not accidentally file the wrong year.

Check the official WASFA page ↗

WSAC is redesigning WASFA for 2027–28. Watch the official page for the new application and instructions.

Washington aid

Washington College Grant & College Bound

Eligible Washington residents can use WASFA to be considered for state aid. College Bound students must complete a financial aid application during senior year and meet the program's other requirements.

Review College Bound ↗

If you are unsure whether you were enrolled in College Bound, ask the College & Career Center to help you check.

Fee assistance

Mission Asset Fund

Mission Asset Fund offers financial tools that can include 0% interest loans for some USCIS filing fees. Covered applications, fees, and state availability can change.

Check eligibility before relying on the program. Do not use the older fee amounts from the original counselor handout.

Health & family support

Washington Healthplanfinder

Washington has health and dental coverage options regardless of immigration status, but financial-help and Apple Health rules are changing.

Current Washington guidance

Health and dental coverage

Washington Healthplanfinder says everyone in Washington can get health and dental insurance regardless of immigration status. You do not need a Social Security number to start an application.

Read immigrant coverage information ↗
Rules are changing

Check before you enroll or renew

Some noncitizen Apple Health rules change October 1, 2026, and federal financial-help eligibility changes again January 1, 2027. State-funded Cascade Care Savings may still be available for people of any immigration status who qualify by income.
