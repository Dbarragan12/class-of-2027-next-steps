Keep it manageable

A simple senior rhythm

Use this as a starting rhythm, then follow the exact dates and requirements from your school, college, program, or employer.

1

Do now

  • Check your school email and senior updates.
  • Choose a first pathway to explore.
  • Start a short list of deadlines.
2

When applying

  • Read the complete requirements first.
  • Request transcripts with processing time.
  • Save confirmations and copies of your work.
3

Before graduation

  • Confirm final records and graduation steps.
  • Complete aid verification and compare costs.
  • Check the new program’s portal and email.

Need support?

For transcript, planning, or application questions, start with the College and Career Center or GEAR UP. Bring the exact school, program, or scholarship link you are working on.
