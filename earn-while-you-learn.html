How it works

Not “college versus success.”

The goal is to match the training model to the career. Some careers require a degree. Others reward students for starting through apprenticeship, technical education, or employer training.

01

Get hired or selected

Registered apprenticeships usually have an application process. Some require age 18, a diploma/GED, transportation, testing, interviews or other prerequisites.

02

Learn on the job

You work under experienced professionals while completing required related instruction. Wages generally increase as skills and hours increase.

03

Finish with a credential

The goal is a recognized skilled-worker credential, license or career qualification — not simply a temporary job.
