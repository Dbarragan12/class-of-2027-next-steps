Scholarship writing

Write once. Adapt many times.

Your story bank

Keep one private document with your activities, jobs, family responsibilities, awards, challenges, and people who shaped you.

The 600-word draft

Write one honest personal statement about a real moment and what changed because of it. Reuse and reshape it instead of starting from zero.

Ask early

Ask recommenders in September. Give them a month, your resume/story bank, and the scholarship deadline.

School, programs & work

Pick a direction. The money follows.

College

  • FAFSA or WASFA is the first step
  • Compare aid offers—not sticker prices
  • Consider YVC transfer routes

Trades & apprenticeships

  • Earn while you learn
  • Explore YVC, Perry Tech, and union programs
  • Ask whether tools and fees are covered

Work & certificates

  • Short credentials can raise your pay quickly
  • Look for paid training and internships
  • Build a resume from jobs, service, and caregiving too
