Default coaching rubric

If the prompt includes its own rubric, the coach should use that instead. Otherwise, it estimates five areas from 0–4 points each.

Prompt fitSpecific evidenceOrganizationVoice & reflectionClarity
Score with context. A number alone is not useful. The coach must explain each rating using the prompt and the student’s actual draft, then give no more than three priority tips.

The coach may

Point out missing parts, confusing sections, repeated ideas, weak transitions, or places where a concrete detail would strengthen the story.

The coach may not

Invent experiences, add achievements, change the student’s identity, or produce a finished essay to submit as the student’s own work.
